# Linkarooie identity and reference creator assets

MP explicitly requested the established Linkarooie identity and his own Loftwah profile. The original portrait, concert kangaroo banner and neon kangaroo app mark are reused byte-for-byte from `loftwah/linkarooie-3` at `149f277bc45baf31eca406878e8e16325b85a06f`, files `src/assets/images/loftwah_avatar.jpg`, `loftwah_banner.jpg` and `icon.png`. No legacy modules, trackers, biography or hidden data execute or import. The Inter fonts come from that same checkout and carry the included SIL Open Font License.

The lime dark/purple light wordmark and typography follow that established identity. Creator images are explicit owner configuration, empty by default. All creators use the same card/settings renderer; Loftwah is a separately configured reference creator.

Cut-paper release, 17 October 2026. The hero illustration, the default social card and the app icon moved to the reviewed App Composer cut-paper screenprint set. `public/brand/linkarooie-art-v3-master.png` is the generated master `tile-linkarooie-v4-1.png` from fal.ai request `01a147de-1cbd-7a03-9297-adf754cdbc42`; its full prompt, rejected revision, cost basis and file inventory are recorded here in `public/brand/linkarooie-art-v3.PROVENANCE.txt` and in the owning App Composer repository. `mark.svg` and `icon.png` are hand-authored chain-ring geometry, not generated or traced from a third-party mark. This release removed the glossy `showcase-character-v2` pair, the generated courtyard/share poster pairs, the 13 October icon and the v1/v2 navigation crops; their bytes remain in git history. No third-party logo, vendor interface or personal image is used in the generated or authored app artwork.

`share-card.html` is the editable 1200 × 630 social-card layout. Replace its PORTRAIT token with the unchanged portrait data URL and render in Chromium to JPEG. The portrait is the original photograph, without generated facial changes. The discarded image-generator experiment was not published. The final card has no stale domain, private biography, invented badges or social platform dependence.

Version 1 settings files retain their original nine fields and preserve the new app-owned image/X configuration when applied; these files are not a full profile/media backup. Existing collection export also includes the image/X settings and all card grouping. Grouped discovery extends the existing cards and presentation rows; no second content store or migration is introduced.
